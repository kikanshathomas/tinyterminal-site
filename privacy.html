Privacy policy

Tiny Terminal: Idle Airport
Effective date: November 17, 2026

Tiny Terminal is a game made by Ubersoft ("we", "us"). This page explains what data the game and this website use, and why. In short: the game itself collects nothing about you. The only data that leaves your phone is what Google's ad service uses when the game requests or shows an ad. This website counts visits separately; see "This website" below.

What the game stores

  • Your game progress (airports, upgrades, planes, coins and settings) is saved on your phone.
  • We have no servers for the game, and we never receive your save.
  • Android may back up your save to your own Google account, so it survives a reinstall or a new phone. That backup is run by Google under your Android backup settings. We can't see it, and you can turn it off in Android settings.
  • There are no accounts and no sign-in. We don't ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos or precise location.
  • The game uses no analytics, crash reporting or tracking tools of its own.

Ads

  • Tiny Terminal is free with ads. It shows two kinds of ads, both from Google AdMob:
    • rewarded videos that you choose to watch for an in-game boost;
    • full-screen ads (interstitials) at natural breaks in play, within limits.
  • These ads are served by Google AdMob. When an ad is requested or shown, Google's ad software collects data from your device and shares it with Google for advertising, analytics (measuring ads) and fraud prevention. The data is sent encrypted. It includes:
    • your device's advertising ID and app set ID;
    • your approximate location (based on your IP address);
    • how you interact with ads, such as which ads you saw and tapped;
    • diagnostics and other app performance data.
  • Google handles this data under its own privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy. How Google uses data from apps that use its services is explained at https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.
  • If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the game shows Google's consent form before any ads, where consent is required. You can change your choice at any time in the game under Settings → Privacy options. If you decline, Google shows non-personalized or limited ads.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads).
  • If you buy Remove Ads, the full-screen ads are turned off and boosts no longer need a video, so the game stops requesting ads.

Purchases

  • Remove Ads is a one-time purchase handled by Google Play.
  • We don't receive your payment details. Google Play tells the game only whether the purchase has been made, so the game can turn off ads and give you the daily boosts.
  • Google's handling of payments is covered by Google Play's terms and Google's privacy policy.

Notifications

The game doesn't send notifications.

This website

This website (tinyterminal.app) is separate from the game, and it does count visits, including visits to this page. It uses GoatCounter, a privacy-friendly analytics service.

  • What is recorded: for each page view, the page, the referring site, your browser and operating system (name and version), your screen size, your language and your approximate country, worked out from your IP address.
  • Where it runs: the counting script loads from gc.zgo.at, GoatCounter's own server.
  • What isn't stored: it sets no cookies and stores nothing in your browser. It doesn't store your IP address or your full browser identification.
  • Repeat visits: to tell a repeat visit from a new one, it holds your IP address and browser identification in memory only, for up to 8 hours, and never writes them to its database.
  • No tracking: it doesn't follow you across other sites and doesn't build a profile of you.
  • Sharing: GoatCounter says it shares no information with third parties. See GoatCounter's privacy policy.

None of this touches the game. Your game progress stays on your phone, and in your phone's own backup if that is on. It is never sent to GoatCounter or to us. The game itself has no analytics, as described above.

Children

Tiny Terminal is meant for players aged 13 and over. It isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them. The game itself collects none from anyone. If you believe a child's data has been processed through ads in the game, contact us and we'll help.

Your choices and rights

  • Delete your game data: uninstall the game, or clear its storage in Android settings. To remove the Android backup copy, manage backups in your Google account.
  • Ad data: use the in-game consent setting, where available, and your Android ad settings. Requests about data held by Google go to Google.
  • Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal data. We hold no personal data about players, but you can contact us with any question.

Changes

If the game's data use changes (for example, if a new feature needs data), we'll update this page and its effective date before that version of the game is released.
