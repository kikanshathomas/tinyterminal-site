Tiny Terminal: Idle Airport

Run a tiny airport. Optional rewarded ads only, and no pop-up ads, ever.

Coming soon to Google Play

Build a small airport into a busy hub, one passenger at a time. Travelers arrive at the curb, check in, drop their bags, pass security, maybe stop at the café, and walk the jet bridge to their seat. When a plane is full, it pushes back and the next one rolls in.

Six station types

Run the floor

  • Upgrade check-in, bag drop, security, the café and the gates
  • Add lanes and staff where the queue builds up
  • Watch every station get richer as it levels up
  • At most three upgrade cards at a time, so the next step is always clear

Three planes

Fill your planes

  • Watch passengers board and take their seats row by row
  • Collect and level up three planes: the quick Hopper, the Swift business jet and the 150-seat Liner
  • Planes are earned with coins, never sold for money

Two airports

Grow your airports

  • Fill flights to open new wings with their own gates
  • Open a route to a second airport, Harbor City, with its own long pier and its own look
  • Older airports keep paying a share of their income through your routes

Play at your own pace

  • Your airport keeps earning while you are away, up to a time limit you can raise
  • Short sessions are fine: check in a few times a day
  • No timers to wait on and no energy system

Ads and purchases, plainly

  • The only ads are rewarded videos that you choose to watch for a short coin boost, up to 12 a day
  • No interstitials and no forced ads, ever
  • One optional purchase, Remove Ads, a one-time purchase: you get the same 12 boosts a day without watching anything
  • No loot boxes, no paid skips and nothing else to buy

No account. Your save stays on your phone.

There are no accounts, no sign-in and no analytics. Your progress is saved on your phone. Read the privacy policy.
