What's inside

A calm, top-down idle game.

Travelers arrive at the curb, check in, drop their bags, pass security, maybe stop at the café, and walk the jet bridge to their seat. When a plane is full, it pushes back and the next one rolls in.

The floor

Run the floor.

Upgrade check-in, bag drop, security, the café and the gates. Add lanes and staff where the queue builds up, and watch every station get richer as it levels up. At most three upgrade cards at a time, so the next step is always clear.

The fleet

Fill your planes.

Watch passengers board and take their seats row by row. Collect and level up three planes: the quick Hopper, the Swift business jet and the 150-seat Liner. Planes are earned with coins, never sold for money.

The airports

Grow your airports.

Fill flights to open new wings with their own gates. Open a route to a second airport, Harbor City, with its own long pier and its own look. Older airports keep paying a share of their income through your routes.

Away time

Play at your own pace.

Your airport keeps earning while you are away, up to a time limit you can raise. Short sessions are fine: check in a few times a day. No timers to wait on and no energy system.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

I bought Remove Ads. How do I get it on a new phone?

Sign in to Google Play on the new phone with the same Google account you bought it with. Then open the game and go to Settings → Restore purchases. Remove Ads is a one-time purchase that turns off pop-up ads, and your 12 daily boosts no longer need a video. You don't pay again.

Where is my progress saved?

On your phone. There are no accounts and no sign-in. Android may back up your save to your own Google account, so it can survive a reinstall or a new phone. That backup follows your Android backup settings.

How do I change my ad privacy choices?

Open the game and go to Settings → Privacy options. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads).

How do I delete my data?

Uninstall the game, or clear its storage in Android settings. We have no servers for the game, so nothing is stored on our side. To remove the Android backup copy, manage backups in your Google account.
